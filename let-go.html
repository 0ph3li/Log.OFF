the ritual · three steps · one sea

let it all go

Every guest does it on the first evening, on the rocks below the house. You write down the thing you're carrying, you crumple it, and you throw it into the sea. It's a bit silly. It works anyway.

    05 · how to let go (a guide)

    1. 01

      name it. out loud, if nobody's listening.

    2. 02

      write it once. no drafts. no backspace.

    3. 03

      make it small. crumple it until it fits in your hand.

    4. 04

      throw it far. further than feels polite.

    5. 05

      don't go back to check. it's not there. that's the point.
