privacy · the honest version

what this
device
remembers.

Nothing you do here leaves your browser. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking pixel pretending to be a gif. But this device does remember a few things, so the site can be a little bit personal. Here they are, every one of them, live. You can make it forget.

    02 · the big button

    forget
    everything.

    Screen time, the notes you threw into the sea, your name, your ticket, how many times you tried to press esc. Hold the button and it all goes. It can't be undone, which is sort of the point of this whole place.

    03 · the small print, in large print

    1. 01

      what we collect

      Nothing. There is no server. LOG.OFF is a static website: it can't send anything anywhere, because there's nowhere to send it.

    2. 02

      what this browser keeps

      A few small things, in localStorage and sessionStorage, all under keys starting with lo-: listed live above. They stay on this device until you forget them here, or clear your browser's site data.

    3. 03

      cookies

      None. Not even the chocolate ones. Sorry.

    4. 04

      analytics & tracking

      None. We don't know you were here. Honestly, that's the most romantic thing about this website.

    5. 05

      other people's servers

      To look the way it does, the site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries (GSAP, Lenis) from public CDNs. Like any website, those services see a normal request from your browser. They don't see anything you type here.

    6. 06

      the forms

      The letters, the postcards, the notes for the sea, the check-in: none of them are sent. The letter you "posted" is still on this device. That's either sad or lovely. We think lovely.

    7. 07

      forgetting

      Use the cards above, or the big button, or type logoff.forget() in the console if that's more your style.
