the program · 7 days · 6 nights · 0 bars

seven days.
less every day.

Every day of the retreat takes something away: a notification, a habit, a reflex, a little bit of noise. By day seven there's almost nothing left. That's the point. Here's how it goes, hour by hour.

01

day 01 · symptom: reaching

withdrawal

You hand it over at the door. It goes into a sealed pink bag, then into a locker with your name on it. For the rest of the day your hand keeps looking for it: in your pocket, on the table, under the pillow. Forty-three times, on average.

  • 09:00arrival. the phone goes in the bag.
  • 09:04first reach. there's nothing there.
  • 13:00lunch, no photos. (the food was fine.)
  • 17:30you ask what time it is. there's a clock on every wall.
  • 23:00you fall asleep without scrolling. it takes a while.

02

day 02 · symptom: buzzing

phantom
vibrations

Your leg buzzes. You check. Nothing. It buzzes again during breakfast, during the walk, during the very quiet part of yoga. We call it the ghost of group chats past. It leaves on its own, usually by the evening.

  • 07:40buzz. nothing.
  • 10:00a walk along the cliff. buzz. nothing.
  • 15:00yoga. the instructor says "notice the urge". you notice it.
  • 21:00you realise it hasn't buzzed in an hour.

03

day 03 · symptom: nothing

boredom

The hardest, holiest day. There is nothing to do and nobody to tell about it. Most guests try to leave on day three. Then, somewhere in the empty afternoon, something grows. Usually an idea. Sometimes a nap.

  • 09:00no plans. on purpose.
  • 11:14you lie down and look at the ceiling.
  • 14:00you want to leave. you don't.
  • 16:20an idea arrives. you have nowhere to post it. you keep it.

00:00

don't touch anything. the clock only moves if you do nothing.

04

day 04 · symptom: wanting proof

the sunset
you didn't film

Everyone goes up to the roof at seven. Everyone's hands do the same thing: they rise, frame the sky, and find nothing to hold. On day four, the sky stops being content and starts being the sky again. It was better than the photo would have been. You'll have to trust us.

  • 18:40the roof. everyone's there. nobody talks.
  • 19:12the sun touches the sea. your hands rise.
  • 19:13your hands come down.
  • 19:30it's gone. you were there for all of it.

05

day 05 · symptom: talking

talking to
strangers

No usernames, no mutuals, no checking someone's profile before you say hi. Just a name, a face, and a very long dinner. You'll forget what most of them looked like online. You'll remember what they sounded like.

  • 08:30name stickers at breakfast. real names only.
  • 12:00a walk with someone you'd never have followed.
  • 20:00the long table. nobody looks down.
  • 02:00still talking.

06

day 06 · symptom: missing someone

letters

You write to someone by hand. No backspace, no read receipts, no typing bubble. It takes four days to arrive, and the waiting is part of it. Some letters are still on their way.

  • 10:00paper, a pen, a stamp. that's the whole interface.
  • 11:30you cross something out. it stays crossed out.
  • 16:00the post van. you wave at it, a bit too much.
